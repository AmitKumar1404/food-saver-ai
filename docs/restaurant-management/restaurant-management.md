# Restaurant Management

## 1. Module Overview

The Restaurant Management module is the first business module
of FoodSaver AI.

It is responsible for managing restaurants/businesses that
participate in the FoodSaver platform.

This module will provide the foundation for later modules such
as:

- Food/Product Management
- Inventory Management
- Surplus Food Detection
- Food Safety Eligibility
- Discount Marketplace
- Customer Ordering
- AI Agents

## 3. Business Requirement

FoodSaver AI needs to maintain restaurant information so that
restaurants can later:

- Register on the platform
- Manage their profile
- Manage food/products
- Maintain inventory
- Identify eligible surplus food
- Create surplus food offers
- Receive customer orders

The Restaurant entity will therefore act as an important
business domain object.
