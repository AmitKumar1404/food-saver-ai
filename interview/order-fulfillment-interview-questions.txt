============================================================
FOODSAVER AI - ORDER FULFILLMENT V1 INTERVIEW QUESTIONS
============================================================

1. BEGINNER

Q1. What does Order Completion V1 mean?
Answer: It changes a CONFIRMED Order to COMPLETED and accounts the held
quantity from Inventory reserved to sold. It does not mean payment, delivery,
pickup verification, refund, or food-safety certification.

Q2. What happens to the source Reservation?
Answer: It remains CONVERTED for audit history.

Q3. Which Inventory fields change?
Answer: reserved decreases and sold increases by the OrderItem quantity.
Available, prepared, and Inventory status remain unchanged.

Q4. Is pricing recalculated at completion?
Answer: No. OrderItem and Reservation snapshots remain authoritative.

2. INTERMEDIATE

Q5. What is the completion-aware outstanding-reserved ledger?
Answer: ACTIVE Reservation quantity plus CONVERTED Reservation quantity linked
to CONFIRMED Orders. Converted Reservations linked to COMPLETED Orders are
excluded.

Q6. Why must orphan CONVERTED Reservations fail closed?
Answer: Without exactly one OrderItem and Order, the service cannot determine
whether their quantity is still outstanding or already sold.

Q7. Why is no completion idempotency key needed?
Answer: The Order row is locked and its lifecycle is the durable idempotency
boundary. CONFIRMED performs the transition; COMPLETED returns unchanged.

Q8. How are completion timestamps produced?
Answer: The server captures one timestamp after all locks, normalizes it to
MySQL TIMESTAMP(6), and uses it for both completedAt and updatedAt.

Q9. Which isolation level is used?
Answer: READ_COMMITTED. Correctness comes from pessimistic row locks,
post-lock validation, database constraints, and one atomic transaction.

3. ADVANCED

Q10. What is the exact completion lock order?
Answer: Customer PESSIMISTIC_WRITE, Order PESSIMISTIC_WRITE, Restaurant
PESSIMISTIC_READ, Product PESSIMISTIC_READ, Inventory PESSIMISTIC_WRITE,
Offers PESSIMISTIC_WRITE in ascending internal ID, then Reservations
PESSIMISTIC_WRITE in ascending internal ID.

Q11. Why is Order locked before Inventory?
Answer: It serializes lifecycle/idempotent replay before quantity mutation and
ensures no completion path acquires Order after entering the shared
Inventory-to-Reservation lock suffix.

Q12. Why is OrderItem not separately locked?
Answer: V1 OrderItem is immutable and uniquely belongs to the locked Order,
but it is still reloaded and fully revalidated inside the transaction.

Q13. What must hold before and after mutation?
Answer: prepared equals available plus reserved plus sold, and Inventory
reserved equals the completion-aware outstanding Reservation ledger.

Q14. What prevents two requests from completing one Order twice?
Answer: The Order write lock serializes them. The winner changes status and
Inventory once; the waiter observes COMPLETED and returns persisted state.

Q15. What prevents two Orders sharing Inventory from losing updates?
Answer: Both must acquire the same Inventory write lock before validating the
current ledger or changing reserved and sold quantities.

4. SCENARIO QUESTIONS

Q16. A failure occurs after both Order and Inventory are changed. What commits?
Answer: Nothing. The transaction rolls back Order to CONFIRMED with null
completedAt and restores Inventory; Reservation remains CONVERTED.

Q17. Completion is retried after a transaction failure. What happens?
Answer: Because the failed transaction committed no lifecycle or quantity
change, the still-CONFIRMED Order can be completed normally.

Q18. Completion is retried after success. What happens?
Answer: The existing completed representation and original completedAt are
returned with no additional Inventory mutation.

Q19. Why are real MySQL tests required?
Answer: Mocks and H2 cannot establish MySQL FOR UPDATE behavior, actual lock
waiting, READ_COMMITTED visibility, TIMESTAMP(6), or production constraint
semantics.

Q20. What must happen before production activation?
Answer: Apply an explicit schema migration and reconcile every converted
Reservation, confirmed outstanding quantity, Inventory equation, and orphan
condition. Do not infer historical sold quantity or silently repair data.

Q21. Is there a Restaurant-scoped fulfillment API in V1?
Answer: No. Completion is Order-scoped through the Customer-owned endpoint
POST /api/v1/customers/{customerPublicId}/orders/{orderPublicId}/complete.
Authentication and authorization remain a production exposure requirement;
public UUID ownership scoping is not authentication.
